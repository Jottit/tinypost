About Tinypost

Tinypost is a radically simple way to write on the web. You sign up, you get a blog, you start writing. No themes, no plugins, no settings to fiddle with.

It's built on a simple belief: that the web should stay easy, open, and personal. There is no tracking, no followers, and no algorithms. Just your words on a small site that is yours.

It's open source, built on web standards, and designed to last. You can see how it works, fork it, or run your own version whenever you like.

If you'd like to support its development or help keep it independent, visit the sponsor page or email hello@tinypost.blog.
